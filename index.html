APD GEN1 S2APD: Bread & Milk

Leadership summary · APD GEN1 Stores · June 18–September 15, 2026

Prioritize Milk S2APD; optimize Bread execution before scaling

Current S2APD completion is low—38% for Milk and 27% for Bread—making transfer execution the primary opportunity.

  • Milk: Transfer activity is associated with improved service and the model shows approximately $47.9K/year savings versus GIF. Prioritize Milk S2APD and improve completion.
  • Bread: Transfer activity improves pre-sub performance, but nil-pick impact is essentially flat and S2APD is currently approximately $15.9K/year more expensive than GIF. Improve productivity before making a broader scale decision.
